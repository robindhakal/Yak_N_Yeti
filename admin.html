How Promotions Work

✅ Overall Promotion
Applies 15% off to everything in the cart. Customer enters code WEB15 at checkout. Best for driving all direct orders.
🎯 Individual Item Promo
Applies discount to specific items only. E.g. "20% off Momos this weekend." Great for slow days or promoting a dish.

Nepalese House Specials

Nepalese Khana Set
The staple food of Nepal consisting of rice, lentil, green mustard leaves (saag), and vegetables (aloo bodi tama) with homemade spicy pickle (achar). Choose between cauliflower(Vegan), green beans(Vegan), chicken curry +2, lamb curry +3, goat curry +4, or fish curry +4. *Vegan: All items vegan if a vegetable is selected as the main curry.
Newari Khaja Set
The Newar community of Nepal have fine tuned their cuisine into some truly magical flavors. This plate represents: chicken sadheko, flattened rice (chiura), roasted soy beans, seasoned potato, a boiled egg, and freshly made pickles (achar). *Vegan option available.
Steamed Momo
Tibetan inspired dumplings enhanced with Himalayan spices and served with special sauces which are perfected for years in the streets of Nepal. Chicken or vegetarian options available. Served with a side of tomato based sauce.
Sadheko Momo
Tibetan inspired dumplings steamed then mixed in fresh slices of chili, ginger, garlic, fresh onion leaves, cilantro, and tomato based sauce creating a freshly unique taste. Chicken or vegetarian options available.
Jhol Momo
Steamed momo served in a bowl of sesame seeded sour and spicy tomato soup. Chicken or vegetarian options available.
Chilly Momo
Deep fried Momo cooked in fresh onions, bell peppers, jalapeño, and special homemade chili sauce. Chicken or vegetarian options available.
Sekuwa
Chicken or lamb +2, marinated in special herbs and spices, then grilled to perfection in tandoor oven.
Chicken Chilly

Paneer Chilly
Cottage cheese cooked in fresh onions, bell peppers, jalapeño, and special homemade chili sauce.
Chow Mein
